RigorQuant turns one Harness session into a lab that explores, verifies, and attacks its own results — running unattended. Nothing is declared done until an independent check says so, on the record. RigorQuant 把一个 Harness 会话变成一座自我探索、自我验证、自我攻击的实验室 —— 全部无人值守地运转。在独立的检查于记录上签字之前，任何事都不算完成。

How it runs如何运转

Not one model. A process that checks itself.不是单个模型，而是一个会自我检查的流程。

Each persona is a separate tool with its own powers and limits, so the producer never checks its own work. The separation is enforced by the composition, not by asking nicely. 每个人格都是一个独立的工具，各有其能力边界，因此生产者绝不自查自己的成果。这种分离由组合本身强制，而不是靠口头约定。

Orchestrator编排者

root persona

Fans out the work, synthesizes, writes the state. Bound by four rules: producer ≠ checker; eliminate only by counterexample; always record seeds; never handwave a load-bearing claim. 扇出工作、综合结果、写状态。受四条铁律约束：生产者≠检查者；只凭反例淘汰；随机运行必记种子；承重命题不许空谈。

Explorer探索者

subagent_explorer

Blank-context, divergent. Proposes lemmas, equations, candidate methods with exact statements. Status reports are rejected. 白纸上下文、刻意发散。给出引理、方程、带精确陈述的候选方法；拒绝状态汇报式的输出。

OffGridThinker离网思考者

subagent_offgrid

Off the grid of other people's results: raw model intelligence plus the compute lane (sympy, numpy, Lean checkers) — no web, no literature, no drafts. Its own agent, not an Explorer variant. 脱离他人结果之网：只凭模型推理与计算通道（sympy、numpy、Lean 校验器）——无联网、无文献、无草稿。独立智能体，不是探索者的变体。

DoubleChecker双重复核

subagent_double_checker

Blind — no web, no skills, no delegation, no drafts. Re-derives the hard claims from first principles, twice by different means. 盲态 —— 无联网、无技能、无委派、无草稿。从第一性原理把关键命题重推两遍，方法各异。

Adversary对抗者

subagent_adversary

Runs the check battery and hunts counterexamples. An idea dies only on a concrete failure — never on style or vibes. Ends in a verdict. 执行检验组、专找反例。一个想法只会死于具体失败案例 —— 绝不因风格或感觉而死，最后给出裁决。

Literature文献线

subagent_lit_line · _adversary

A walled citation-graph sweep, then an independent adversary re-retrieves each claim and certifies it's real and current. 封闭式引文图遍历，再由独立对抗者逐条重取主张，确认其真实且不过时。

Validator校验器

rq_check.py + schemas

Refuses a PASS with missing evidence. Reads the audit record, never the study's own claims — a study cannot vouch for itself. 证据缺失即拒绝 PASS。只读审计记录，绝不读研究自称的主张 —— 研究无法为自己作保。

the loop — how the personas hand off循环 —— 人格之间如何交接
01

Promise narrowly按原意承诺

Record the original question verbatim, split into sub-problems with crisp success criteria, pick hand-checkable simplified cases, pin seeds, tolerances and the schema/validator digests. 逐字记录原始问题，拆成带明确成功判据的子问题，挑手算可验的简化情形，钉死种子、容差以及 schema／校验器的摘要。

orchestrator writes the contract编排者写下契约
02

Fan out扇出

Explorers and literature lines run on blank context. Most are never told the favored approach. 探索者与文献线在白纸上下文中运行；大多数不会被告知偏好的路线。

→ explorers propose探索者提出
03

Ground-truth it求真

The blind DoubleChecker re-derives the load-bearing claims without seeing anyone's draft. Two independent derivations for anything the study rests on. 盲态的双重复核不看草稿地重推承重命题；凡研究赖以立足之处，必须有两份独立推导。

isolated · blind隔离 · 盲态
04

Attack it攻击

The adversary runs the four-gate battery, then hunts counterexamples. Divergent tracks are lined up as an adjudication docket first. 对抗者先跑四关检验，再找反例。分歧的轨道会先被排成一份裁定案卷。

+ → adversary attacks both tracks对抗者攻击两条轨道
05

Certify & ship认证并交付

The validator checks nothing is missing; the paper and slides are assembled from validated records, not written fresh. 校验器确认无遗漏；论文与幻灯由已验证记录装配，而不是现写。

Honesty is tested诚实性被测试

A framework whose honesty gate isn't tested will certify whatever it's handed. 诚实闸门本身不被测试的框架，会认证任何递给它的东西。

So the test suite includes a forged study — empty derivations, a one-line audit, a paper that says nothing — and it must fail. 因此测试套件里含有一份伪造研究 —— 空的推导、一行审计、一篇「什么都没说」的论文 —— 而它必须失败。
